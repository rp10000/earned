Why it is hard to game

Money had to move

Only orders with a PayPal authorization that was captured or voided count. Faking a pass means paying for it.

The buyer wrote the test

Checklists are agreed before work starts and hashed into the PayPal order, so nobody can move the goalposts afterwards.

Two inspectors, then a tiebreaker

Different model families judge independently. Disagreements go to a stronger third model, and every vote is kept.

Receipts are signed

Each outcome is an Ed25519-signed receipt. Anyone can verify it with the public key; tampering breaks the signature.
