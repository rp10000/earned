How it works

The price is decided by the result, not the seller.

Every step is a real PayPal call: an Orders v2 authorization, a partial capture with final_capture, a void when nothing passed, a Payouts batch to the studio, refunds for upheld appeals, and webhooks that confirm each one.

Agree the checklist

AI turns "a card for my cats" into 3–6 checkable facts: exact text, counts, colours, exclusions. You edit it. Its hash is written into the PayPal order.

PayPal holds the money

An authorization, not a charge. The studio knows the money is real; you know it hasn't moved.

Two AI inspectors check every result

Two different vision models judge each image item by item and quote their evidence. If they disagree, a third, stronger model decides.

PayPal captures only what passed

3 of 4 passed? You pay for 3; the rest of the hold is released. A signed receipt records the outcome forever.

The studio gets paid

PayPal Payouts sends the studio its share minus a 10% fee. If an appeal is upheld later, you are refunded at once and the studio repays it from its next payout.

Why it matters

AI is the first product that is sold before anyone knows whether it worked.

Buyers hesitate

We run small AI tools. Our paying customers arrive from chat assistants on their phones, and they stall at the same moment: paying before they have seen the result. Free previews help; a guarantee that is enforced by the payment itself helps more.

Refunds are binary and slow

Today a bad result means a refund request, a dispute, or nothing. Most results are not all bad; they are 3 of 4 good. Pay-per-pass settles that in seconds, in proportion, with evidence.

Agents can't complain

When an AI agent buys from another AI, no human is watching. The only workable rule is: verify, then settle. Earned is that rule as a protocol, running on PayPal.

What makes it different

Not escrow, not a dispute desk. Inspection before payment.

Checked before anyone pays

Escrow and dispute tools step in after a deal goes wrong and decide who is right. Earned checks every delivery against the agreed checklist before money moves, so a fair price never depends on someone complaining.

Per item, not all-or-nothing

Most AI output is 3 of 4 good. One PayPal partial capture charges for exactly the 3 and releases the rest. Facts decide the price; taste is reported but never billed.
